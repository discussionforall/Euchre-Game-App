Euchre — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026 Developer: Sparkle Infotech Apps

Overview

Euchre ("the App") is a single-player card game in which you and a computer partner play the classic trick-taking game Euchre against two computer opponents, first to 10 points. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the App and how it's handled.

The short version: we collect nothing, the App sends nothing anywhere, and your game data stays on your device. There are no ads, no analytics and no account.

Information We Collect

We collect nothing. The App has no account creation, no sign-in and no password. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any device identifiers.

The App contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party services that collect data of any kind.

Information Stored On Your Device

The App saves a small amount of information locally so it can remember it between sessions. It never leaves your device and we have no access to it:

Your record — the number of games you have played, won and lost, and your current and best winning streaks.

Settings — the speed at which the computer players act (Normal or Fast).

A game in progress is kept only while the App is open, so you can step back to the home screen and continue it. It is not saved, and it is gone once the App is closed.

How We Use This Information

We don't, because we never receive it. The information above is read and written by the App on your own device, purely to run the game: to show your record on the home screen and to remember your speed setting. None of it is transmitted, profiled, sold or shared with anyone.

Where This Information Is Stored

In the App's private storage area on your phone, which other apps cannot read. There is no account, no cloud sync and no server-side copy. If you install the game on a different device, it starts fresh.

Permissions

The App never asks you to grant a permission, and it works without any.

The Android package declares a few standard permissions that the framework the App is built with includes by default, such as Internet access. None of them is used to send or receive any information about you.

The App does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, photos or notifications.

Network Access

The App makes no network requests. It is fully playable offline and in aeroplane mode. The computer players run entirely on your phone. Nothing about you or your game leaves your phone, because the App never contacts a server — not ours, and not anyone else's.

There are no ads to load, no analytics to report, no update checks and no leaderboards.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

There are none. The App shows no adverts, uses no advertising service, and contains no analytics, attribution, crash-reporting or A/B-testing service. It does not read your Advertising ID. No third party receives any information about you through this App.

Purchases

There are none. The whole game is free to play and the App asks for no payment information.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. It contains no adverts, no purchases, no chat and no way for players to contact one another — every other player at the table is controlled by the App.

We collect no personal information from anyone, which means we collect none from children.

Your Choices

Everything the App stores is on your device and under your control. You can clear your record at any time using Settings → Reset record inside the App, or remove everything by uninstalling the App, which permanently deletes its local storage.

Because we hold no data about you, there is no account to close and no data request for us to fulfil.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. If a future version adds a feature that involves data — such as advertising — this page will be updated to describe it before that version is released.
